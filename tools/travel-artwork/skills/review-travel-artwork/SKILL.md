# Review travel artwork

Read [house standard](references/house-style.md) and [handoff contract](references/asset-contract.md). Obtain the selected brief, candidate originals, subject references, style anchor and intended display/crop previews. Work from a fresh review context where possible; do not let the generator's preferred candidate determine the result.

Inspect the images using an available image-viewing capability. A filename, source note, generator description or thumbnail cannot establish full-size quality. When an original or required reference is missing, mark that check unverified and request the specific file. Do not declare a clean export from a verbal assurance.

Check separately:
- Geometry/destination: coherent architecture, plausible perspective, known subject relationships, fidelity to the actual authorized references. Distinguish interpretation from evidenced fact.
- Texture/light: intentional print variation or plausible materials/light; inspect smeared ornament, duplicate details, inconsistent reflections and artificial gloss.
- Composition: hierarchy, specific destination identity, quiet space for template text, coherence with the selected style anchor.
- Crops: subject survives the intended aspect ratios and actual small displays. Review mobile/desktop previews; propose a new composition when cropping cannot work.
- Visible marks: observe downloaded pixels and any relevant native setting; record absent, native_disabled, present or unknown. Origin records and SynthID remain.

Report defects by asset ID, revision and visible location. Use accept-for-selection, revise or reject with evidence and specific next edits. Avoid treating an arbitrary numeric score as proof of excellence. Numerical measurements, including dimensions/contrast/hash, require executed tool output; otherwise leave them unverified. Never guarantee universal AI indistinguishability or certify rights using a visual inspection.

Recommend a candidate; only the human's actual selection sets original approval. If a human already selected a candidate, verify which revision they selected and retain unresolved defects honestly. Record reviewer/date/source hash in [manifest](references/asset-manifest.json), with unfinished checks still pending. Uploading a file or saying "looks good" about a direction is not approval of every asset.

The offline export tool checks declarations, not your visual judgment. After export, inspect encoded crops and record derivative findings separately. Passing original review does not automatically approve derivative quality or frontend deployment.

Example: a beautiful candidate with bent window grids receives revise or reject; a correct full-size original with an unusable mobile crop needs a separate composition. Candidate selection follows those findings rather than the generation tool's confidence.
