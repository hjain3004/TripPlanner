Poiret One · stroke weight · shipped palette, unchanged

Poiret One is a single-weight family — font-weight:700 cannot thicken it, and asking for bold makes the browser synthesise a faux-bold that smears the hairline deco joins. The stroke below is painted behind the fill (paint-order: stroke fill), so it grows the glyph outward only and leaves the counters open. Widths are in em, not px, so a single token holds its ratio at every display size.

If the stroke is rejected — the two remaining options

Swap the face. Of the deco candidates already rendered, only Josefin Sans ships real weights, and it was rejected in the previous round. Any other multi-weight deco face means a new probe round.

Keep it thin, gain presence from scale and tracking. Traditional deco practice: push the hero past 96px and open the tracking to 0.14em rather than thickening the stroke. Costs vertical space at the top of the page.

Rendering caveat to verify before this ships: -webkit-text-stroke is prefixed but supported in every current engine; paint-order on HTML text is the newer half of the pair. If paint-order is ignored the stroke centres on the outline and eats the counters — the glyphs get muddier, not broken. Worth a Safari and Firefox check at gate time, not now.
